## 🚀 Quick Start: Install in 60 Seconds

You don't need Arduino IDE, Python, or command-line tools to get started:

1. Connect your **ESP32-2432S028R ("Cheap Yellow Display")** to your computer via USB.
2. Open the official **[Bipkins Web Flasher](https://bipkins.github.io/firmware/)** in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
3. Click **Install Bipkins** and select your USB serial port.
4. Once flashed, your pet will hatch immediately on the CYD color display!

---

## ⚡ Hardware & System Features

- **Fluid 60 FPS Animation Engine:** Hardware double-buffered graphics pipeline on the ILI9341 320×240 display with zero dropped frames.
- **On-Device Touch Keyboard & QR Captive Portal:** Effortlessly configure WPA2 Wi-Fi directly on the touch screen, or connect using your smartphone.
- **Dual-Connectivity (USB Internet Bridge):** Fully playable untethered via Wi-Fi or tethered via USB with instant clock synchronization.
- **True Real-Time Care & Progression:** Cryptographically validated time sync prevents clock manipulation; your companion grows through hatch, baby, and adult stages naturally.
- **Economy & Interaction:** Pet, feed, clean, and play with your companion with resistive touch hitboxes, tactile sound, and minigames.
